## Editorial flags before owner approval

1. **28/30 are ELITE or above.** That is plausible for a hand-curated collection of historically famous GKs, but the pool is heavily concentrated in elite tiers. Do not invent weak cards merely to populate ACE.
2. **Career tier does not always equal current OVR.** Review any ELITE card whose current OVR matches or exceeds a LEGEND. The engine currently relies on individual ratings and class, not a hidden tier boost.
3. **Distribution and Sweeping are meaningful now.** Review particularly Higuita, Campos, Ederson, Neuer, Barthez, Chilavert and van der Sar to ensure distinct styles, rather than merely decorative classes.
4. **Do not auto-promote modern stars to TITAN.** TITAN currently means historically exceptional across eras. The historical comparison requires human editorial approval.
5. **No goalkeeper rating or tier was altered in T3-8.** The new feature only changes how the two relevant attributes influence matches.
