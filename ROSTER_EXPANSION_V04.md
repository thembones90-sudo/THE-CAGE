## Editorial method

The six base values are independent prototype estimates for a prime-career abstraction. Scale anchors: 50 limited for this task at this level; 70 capable; 85 specialist; 95 exceptional. These are football-model judgments, not verified historical measurements. Each new record has a short role rationale. Additional fields retain the existing transparent proxy method: first touch and vision average technique/passing; shooting averages finishing/physicality; stamina averages pace/physicality; positioning averages defence/passing; composure averages technique/finishing. The engine currently consumes six base values plus proxy stamina; unused extra fields are not represented as independently calibrated features.

## Search

Exact canonical identities and researched aliases resolve only after submission. Punctuation and accents normalize consistently. Surnames and token prefixes of at least four letters offer up to four matching supported full identities after Enter/Search; broader queries ask for more text. Common multi-person surnames display a namesake warning and require explicit identity confirmation rather than silently burning a pick. Known unsupported identities return a specific unsupported response; unknown text states the 63-player coverage limit, not that the person does not exist. Tight one-edit corrections require at least six characters and display at most two near matches after submission. R9 is reserved for Ronaldo Nazário. The same canonical id remains exhausted under every alias.
